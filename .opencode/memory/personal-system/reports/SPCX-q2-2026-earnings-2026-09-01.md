## TL;DR — Top Call

> **Rating: 1-star (AVOID) at current levels; Neutral on existing core position.**
>
> Q2 was an **operationally strong print that the market has correctly decided to fade**: revenue +92% YoY, Adj EBITDA +191% YoY, all three segments profitable on Adj-EBITDA basis. But **Capex of $18.4B in a single quarter** (vs $2.8B a year ago) means the company is consuming capital faster than it generates it — operating cash flow $3.5B H1 vs Capex $28.5B H1. Combined with a $60B all-stock **Cursor acquisition** announced in the quarter, **$20B bridge loan refinancing**, dual-class structure (Musk ~85% voting control), and a **June 2027 lockup expiry** that unlocks 95% of the float, the asymmetric setup is **downside skew**. Morningstar explicitly held FV at $62 and called the stock "around twice our fair value estimate" after-hours on Aug 4.

### Read on the print

- **On reported revenue/EBITDA: a beat.** Morningstar's pre-print model (set in the Jun 16 initiating note) anticipated "92% growth" — actual was 92%. They did not adjust their forecast after the print. This is **not a beat vs the published estimate**; it is **in-line execution**.
- **On cash conversion: a serious yellow flag.** Adj EBITDA of $3.5B was more than offset by $18.4B of Q2 Capex (8.6B Space + Connectivity + 15.8B AI). Operating cash flow for H1 2026 was only $3.47B against $28.5B of Capex — **a $25B H1 free-cash-flow gap funded by the $85.7B IPO + $25B bond proceeds**.
- **On AI Adj EBITDA flip**: went from $(609)M Q1 → +$1,146M Q2. But the underlying loss from operations was still $(1,257)M. The "EBITDA" gap is depreciation ($1,885M) + SBC ($516M). **Removing SBC and D&A from Adj EBITDA inflates profitability that doesn't exist in cash terms** — a critical non-GAAP caveat called out in the 8-K Note 1.
- **On net loss headline**: $541M headline loss is small in absolute terms but represents only ~3% improvement on revenue growth of 92%. **Operating leverage is unimpressive ex-AI non-cash add-backs.**

## 4. AI Segment — Critical Examination

### 4.1 Is the $14.1B in CSAs real demand or Musk-circle recirculation?

Cross-reference: [Morningstar Jun 16 note](https://www.morningstar.com/integrations/mcp-redirects/company-report/1485126) confirms the Anthropic deal at **$1.25B/month for up to three years** = $45B maximum if fully drawn. **$14.1B in "contracted sales"** is plausibly the cumulative booked portion as of Q2 close.

**Open question is who the counterparties are.** Of $14.1B:
- ~$15B theoretical ceiling from just Anthropic (if all 12 months × $1.25B were recognized) — but this is multi-year and only partially recognized
- Other named counterparties in the print: unnamed "several Cloud Services Agreements"
- **Critical risk: xAI (now merged into SpaceX) was itself buying compute capacity pre-merger from SpaceX → now eliminated as revenue but may still have related-party economics**

**The Cursor deal — $60B announced for Q3 close** — is the bigger concern. $60B for an AI coding tool represents **~3.2% of the company's market cap** absorbed into a non-cash-generating, low-moat AI coding product. The deal terms are unconfirmed in the print (cash vs stock vs mix), but the magnitude is concerning:

| Concern | Implication |
|:--|:--|
| Cursor competes directly with GitHub Copilot, Anthropic Claude Code, etc. — none of which have a moat | Value-destructive M&A in a non-moat segment |
| $60B is ~12× the entire FY2026 estimated AI segment revenue | Massive multiple paid |
| Not arm's-length — Musk has conflicts across xAI/Anthropic/now Cursor via influence | Related-party risk |
| If funded with stock, dilutive; if funded with cash, balance sheet stress | Either way, value-leakage risk |

**Morningstar's verdict** (verbatim): "We don't think that the development of agentic workflows creates meaningful switching costs for enterprises." So the $60B paid for Cursor is largely a bet on Cursor's pricing power, which Morningstar explicitly does not see.

### 4.2 Can 247% growth persist?

**Deceleration math**:
- H2 2026 base effect: Q2 2025 AI revenue was only $737M, so a similar +200% growth in H2 2026 requires revenue ~$2.2B/quarter, which is BELOW Q2 2026 already. **Mechanical deceleration is locked in.**
- Total addressable market constraint: 1.4 GW of compute today; the Anthropic-style deal can max out at ~$15B annualized if fully utilized. **The $14.1B "contracted sales" is approaching the natural ceiling** of disclosed capacity.

**Verdict on durability**: The 247% growth print is **the peak YoY print** in absolute growth-rate terms. Sequential growth Q1→Q2 was +213% — already decelerating. Expect **sequential growth to drop to ~50-80% by Q4 2026** as base effects kick in.

### 4.3 Is $60B Cursor additive or value-destructive?

**Likely value-destructive** for three reasons:
1. Morningstar assigns **zero moat** to consumer AI / coding AI products
2. The 12× revenue multiple is multiples of what public SaaS comps trade at
3. The deal appears to be **not arm's-length** given Musk's cross-entity control

**The single largest unaddressed question in the print** is the deal currency (cash vs stock vs mix). If **all-stock at $143.69**, that's ~418M shares (~3.2% dilution); if **all-cash**, the company would consume ~half its $100B cash pile. Either outcome is meaningful.
